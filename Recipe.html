Mo:Mo

Its popular in Nepal.

Ingredients

  • Flour-2/3 cups
  • Meat/Vegetables- 250gm
  • Salt- 1 tsp
  • Oil- for frying
  • Spices- if needed
  • Water- as much needed

Instructions

  1. Step 1 : Prepare dough.
  2. Step 2 : Prepare filling using ingredients.
  3. Step 3 : Make according to shape as you want. Make round or other shapes.
  4. Step 4 : Now put all in steamer.
  5. Step 5 : Cook until it is cooked.

Pizza

Its an italina dish used with dough base. Adding pizza sauce, cheese and other what is required.

Ingredients

  • Flour - 3 cups
  • Meat - toppings
  • Cheese - 1 slice
  • Pizza sauce - 2 tbsp

Instructions

  1. Step 1: prepare a dough
  2. Step 2: Prepare pizza base
  3. Step 3: Add pizza sauce round over it
  4. Step 4: Add cheese and other toppings like meat/vegetable
  5. Step 5: Bake it in the oven

Chowmein

Its a classic chinese dish which is used to add some vegetable or meat.It is also used to add some sauce for its taste.

Ingredients

  • Noodles -200gm
  • Vegetables/Meat- 250gm
  • Soy sauce-2 tbsp
  • Salt- half tsp
  • Spices- as needed

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Boil noodles
  2. Step 2: Prepare vegetables and meat which to add
  3. Step 3: Add soy sauce, salt and spices
  4. Step 4: Mix all together
  5. Step 5: Serve it hot

Burger

Its a fast food dish. It is made with bun and added inside with patty, vegetables and cheese.

Ingredients

  • Bun -2 pieces
  • Meat patty - 1 piece
  • Cheese - 1 slice
  • Tomato/Onion - topping
  • Sauce - 1/2 tbsp

Instructions

  1. Step 1: Prepare the buns
  2. Step 2: Cook the meat patty properly
  3. Step 3: Add patty, cheese and vegetables.
  4. Step 4: Add sauce around the buns
  5. Step 5: Serve the burger hot
